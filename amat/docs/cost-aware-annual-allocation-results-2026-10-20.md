# Cost-aware yearly allocation updates

Completed October 20, 2026. Neither fixed cost-aware variant passes the historical frontier at every cost. No candidate promoted or added to active profiles; the verified current default remains unchanged.

At each prior-year cutoff, replay the saved proposed recipe and the previously accepted settings over a fixed trailing 252 or 756 observed sessions. Both native counterfactuals start flat at $100,000 with earlier signal warm-up. Accept the proposal only if its net return at the scenario cost exceeds the previous settings by an additional two-sided one-portfolio-turn cost hurdle: 0.04/0.12/0.20 percentage points at 2/6/10 bps. Rejected changes retain the previous accepted settings. The full evaluation itself is one continuous account without annual resets.

Both variants retain the smaller-position quality parent’s 40% symbol cap, quality filter and 2% trading buffer. Saved recipes and masks are unchanged. No new fitted model, symbolic overlay or exit overlay is introduced. This is a fixed native autoresearch study; no hosted proposal/review was used.

## Decision and interpretation

The one-year screen rejects all nine proposed changes and is economically identical to the unchanged quality parent at all three costs. It avoids the low-cost loss of unconditional yearly updates, but also misses their 6/10-bps gains. There is no new strategy improvement to retain.

The three-year screen accepts the 2025 recipe at 2/6 bps, and the 2026 recipe at 6 bps. The realized full-history return falls to 296.36% at 2 bps and 278.40% at 6 bps. At 10 bps it makes no updates and matches the parent. Past counterfactual advantage did not reliably transfer to the next year. No additional variants were invented after seeing these outcomes.

All candidates fail the expanded per-cost full/forward return frontier, which includes stronger rejected annual-quality candidates. Shared-contract coverage also remains incomplete for the broader 38-asset and signed-sleeve source studies. No overall-best claim or promotion is allowed.
