## Each wake

1. Read protocol, state, latest findings and earlier rejected evidence. Check UTC deadline first. If expired, do not start new research or promote; finish/checkpoint already-running measurement safely, produce the final report and pause this heartbeat.
2. Verify no campaign writer/measurement is active before editing or launching a new batch. Resume pending validation rather than duplicate its writer. Use a campaign file lock and record process/run identity if launching a background measurement; release the lock only when the writer finishes. Twenty minutes is a wake cadence, not permission to overlap or truncate tests.
3. Audit all prior source-produced strategies/candidates, including rejected arms. Index protocol/record/source/data hashes, recipes, dates, cash, costs, fees, volume policy and validity. Canonical duplicate session histories may share one comparator but cannot disappear from the audit. Build the per-cost full-history and forward-return frontier. Treat noncomparable histories, execution models, datasets and fitting protocols explicitly; remeasure meaningful prior strategies under a shared contract before any overall-best promotion. A known-invalid attempt is not a valid benchmark. Never remove a stronger comparator because it previously failed another guard.
4. Choose one NEW falsifiable mechanism from previous diagnostics. Preregister it before outcomes, with expected benefit, preserved invariants, fixed variants and acceptance criteria. ## Completion

If fewer than twenty minutes remain, treat that wake as the final scheduled cycle: do not launch work that cannot finish in the remaining time, checkpoint pending work, write the final campaign comparison and pause the heartbeat before returning. Do not rely on a wake after the finite schedule expires. At expiry, launch no new research or promotion. Pause the heartbeat when completion is detected and report candidates tested, valid/rejected/incomplete counts, promotions and final selected default, return/drawdown/cost/stress comparisons, independent useful engineering work and limitations.
