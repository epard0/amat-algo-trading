**Entry-quality ML and adverse-selection proxies are implemented, but this experiment did not establish a performance improvement.** The optional model preserves the baseline portfolio and learns when to reduce a planned addition. The default settings stayed at baseline entry size throughout the measured history. A predeclared control that pools regime evidence produced a small later-period gain, with an uncertainty interval that includes deterioration. The baseline trading configuration remains the reference.

The implementation adds four causal forecast heads: 60-session net return, five-session adverse excursion after the entry window, the relative value of waiting until the following opening window, and a lower-quartile timing forecast. Inputs include short and medium trend, path efficiency, volatility shock, relative volume, candle close location, opening gaps, recent window-to-close returns and a dollar-volume illiquidity measure. Waiting labels include both avoided losses and missed favorable entries.

The model can reduce an addition by at most 50% for one consecutive session under the initial recipe. It restores baseline entry size when the budget is exhausted, when data or predictions are unavailable, when features move outside the training range, or when the relevant regime lacks enough historical support. A recent check of matured counterfactual outcomes can withdraw permission when warnings start missing winners. The baseline retains control of exits and portfolio limits. These mechanisms are independently removable in the research controls.

These later dates had already been inspected in earlier research. They are not an untouched holdout. Each delay changes the actual scheduled execution session; it is not merely a stale signal. Costs remain an explicit fixed stress assumption, not measured spreads or realized price impact.

The default candidate made zero size reductions. During development, no regime accumulated two sufficiently populated validation folds. There were 779 matured baseline entry opportunities, including only 50 in stressed conditions. Of 13,849 measured asset-session decisions, 3,974 warnings were rejected for insufficient regime evidence, 1,068 encountered unfamiliar features, five lacked complete predictions, and 8,802 had no timing edge above the threshold. The model fitted 80 forecast heads across development refits; it was inactive because the policy required evidence, not because training was skipped.

The pooled-regime control changed 14 recorded buy decisions over the full history. Its later-period CAGR gain was about 4.6 basis points per year. Sharpe increased from 1.30764 to 1.31743, but the trial-budget-adjusted bootstrap lower bound for the Sharpe difference was −0.01939. The corresponding guards also failed under higher costs and longer execution lags. No development-period benefit was observed. Removing the uncertainty penalty reduced development CAGR from 3.9064% to 3.9035% and later CAGR from 7.5088% to 7.5001%. Removing the adverse-risk term or recent health check did not change the default portfolio, so this sample does not establish their incremental value.

The model's feedback now tells the next research proposer whether a trial was active, how often it intervened, which fallback reasons occurred, and how many refits had sufficient regime evidence. This lets the next experiment distinguish sparse evidence from negative measured results. It does not treat a successful fit or unchanged performance as added value.

Execution verification covered 14 comparison/control runs and five additional persisted active-control runs. Checks covered observed execution windows and raw prices, modeled costs, entry permissions, the 1% window-volume participation limit, nonnegative cash and the accounting identity. All passed. Actual historical size reductions were exercised at one-, two- and three-session lags. All 559 recorded training-label maturity checks passed. Appending later data preserved earlier model features, predictions, decisions, cash, fills and attribution exactly. Terminal next-decision allocation diagnostics are omitted by a truncated backtest when no following execution session exists; this does not change its ledger.

Unit regressions additionally exercise partial fills, forced sales after a reduced buy, missing windows, adjusted-price labels, exclusion of potentially pre-entry daily lows, future-data mutation, uncertainty penalties, insufficient regime evidence, missed-winner feedback, and the deferral budget. The isolated final suite passed 1,885 tests with one expected dependency-layer skip. Lint and strict type checks passed for the changed research modules. The final development and later-history replays reproduced every saved model array, equity, cash and fill exactly. Later-period Decimal attribution differed by at most 3E-25 currency units. Test results, source provenance and replay comparisons are recorded with the evidence bundle.

The saved data contains daily candles and opening-window VWAP/volume. These support adverse-move and liquidity proxies. They do not support quoted-spread measurement, order-book imbalance, queue modeling, or informed-order-flow identification. True short-horizon adverse-selection measurement needs synchronized quotes/trades and subsequent midpoint markouts; see the [SEC's Rule 605 FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/trading-markets-frequently-asked-questions/frequently-asked-questions-rule-605-regulation-nms). A lower-quartile tree forecast is also not a calibrated confidence interval.
